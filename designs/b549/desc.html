The Story

HE WANTED OUT. NOW HE WANTS IN. A funny dog dad shirt for the man who has opened the same door nine times before breakfast, for Father's Day, a birthday or Christmas, signed from the dog.

The vintage screen-print scene shows a doorman in a peaked cap, buttoned coat and gloves bending in a formal bow as he holds a tall open door, a scruffy mixed-breed dog sitting in the doorway at his feet, a porch step and a patch of lawn, in mustard, brick red, cream and dark brown inks with a worn finish. HE WANTED OUT. sits above the scene and NOW HE WANTS IN. below it in tall condensed capitals, cream and mustard on the dark shirts with a cream edge around the art, dark brown and rust on the light ones.

It is a lightweight unisex tee in nine colors, easy to wear for the morning walk and out. Original art, made for Graphic Tees Co. and printed the day you order it.
