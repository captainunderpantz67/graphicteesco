The Story

I don't run, I throw bags: a cornhole shirt for the backyard and tailgate player who lunges like a sprinter for a toss that travels twelve feet, worn to the cookout, the driveway tournament and every summer birthday.

The print is a 1950s gym-poster illustration of an athlete in a red headband, red shirt, cream shorts and striped knee socks, stretched into a full sprinter's lunge with a checkered bean bag in his raised hand, a slanted wooden cornhole board with a round hole and a patch of mustard grass beneath him. I DON'T RUN. runs across the top in heavy slab capitals and I THROW BAGS. closes the print along the bottom, a little wider.

Wear it to the tailgate, the lake house or any lawn where the boards come out, or give it to the friend who has never once admitted that the walk to retrieve the bags was the only real exercise. Original art, made for Graphic Tees Co. and printed the day you order it.
