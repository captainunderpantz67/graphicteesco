# Ecommerce search backtrack: Oct 2024 to Oct 2026

Researched 2026-10-16. This covers what Google, Microsoft/Bing, OpenAI, Perplexity, Shopify and schema.org published or changed between October 2024 and October 2026 that matters to an online apparel store, checked against the live graphicteesco.com.

**What I checked on the live site (2026-10-16):** the homepage, `/christmas-shirts/`, `/products/womens-christmas-sleigh-queen-graphic-tee/`, `/products/womens-western-desert-bloom-graphic-tee/` (a $24.99 sale item), `/shipping-returns/`, `/feeds/products.xml`, `/robots.txt`, `/llms.txt`, `/sitemap-index.xml` and `/sitemap-0.xml`. I also read `src/lib/schema.ts` and confirmed the IndexNow script and workflow exist. I changed nothing in the repo except this file.

**What the site looks like right now:**
- **Product JSON-LD:** each product has one `Product` with one `AggregateOffer` (for example `lowPrice` = `highPrice` = 29.99 across 66 variants) and an offer-level `MerchantReturnPolicy`. There is no `sku`, `color`, `size`, `shippingDetails` or `priceValidUntil`, and no strikethrough price.
- **Organization JSON-LD:** `OnlineStore`, with no return or shipping policy attached.
- **No visible sale pricing:** no product or collection page shows a "was" price or a strikethrough. The Desert Bloom page shows only "$24.99".
- **Feed:** 6,617 variant rows across 158 products, with `item_group_id`, `color`, `size`, `gender`, `age_group`, `identifier_exists=no` and `google_product_category=212`. `price` holds the current price (24.99 on sale items). There is no `sale_price`, `shipping`, `material` or `lifestyle_image_link`, and none of the 2026 conversational attributes.
- **Returns policy page vs markup:** the page says "we can't take returns for size or change of mind". Only misprinted or damaged items get a reprint or refund, and the customer sends a photo. The markup says "30-day window, free return by mail, full refund or exchange", limited to `DamagedCondition`.
- **Sitemap:** 188 URLs, with no `lastmod` and no image entries.
- **robots.txt:** allows every AI crawler. llms.txt exists.
- **Images:** cover and lifestyle images (`/covers/*.webp`, 600×800) have no IPTC or C2PA AI metadata. The main feed image is 1000×1000.

**Unverified items:**
- I couldn't load OpenAI's merchant sign-up page (chatgpt.com/merchants returned 403).
- The Microsoft Copilot shopping and Perplexity Merchant Program details come only from third-party coverage.
- I didn't check whether Search Console shipping and returns settings are configured, or whether Shopify Catalog is sending this headless store's products to AI channels and which URLs it uses.
- Rows marked "unverified" weren't confirmed from a primary source.

Impact key:
- **High:** blocks eligibility, or puts wrong information in front of shoppers.
- **Med:** a real improvement in visibility or conversion.
- **Low:** hygiene, or optional.

## Top 15 fixes (ranked)

1. **Replace `AggregateOffer` with a single `Offer`** in `src/lib/schema.ts`. Product pages are currently ineligible for Google merchant listings.
2. **Fix the return policy so the markup matches the page.** The markup advertises 30-day free returns by mail, but the policy page says no returns except misprints and damage. Use `MerchantReturnNotPermitted`, or rewrite the policy, and keep the two consistent.
3. **Connect Google Merchant Center** and add `/feeds/products.xml` as a daily scheduled fetch.
4. **Set shipping and returns in Search Console** (or Merchant Center): US, free at $45+, $4.95 under, handling and transit days, and the same return policy as the page. These settings override the markup.
5. **Make the sale real everywhere or nowhere.** Either show "$24.99, was $29.99" on product and collection pages, or stop calling it a sale. US free listings only annotate sales when both prices are on the landing page.
