# How to research a design before it gets art (2026-10-17)

Sam: "do all the research possible to make sure we land these as hard as possible." Every brief records all four:

1. **Search demand** – Google volume/KD (rulebook R11), plus RankHero Etsy volume for taste.
2. **Sales proof in the same format** – one of:
   - TikTok Shop sold count: open `https://shop.tiktok.com/us/k/<keyword-with-dashes>` in Chrome, wait 5 s; the product grid loads behind the "verify" box (never solve it), read "N sold". Many slugs 404; use the page's "Related Searches".
   - Etsy favorites: `python3` RankHero scrape (see docs/research/etsy-2026-10-17/*.json for the format), 500+ favorites in the same format.
3. **Insider proof** – the joke uses a word or bit the community really says. TikTok topic pages `https://www.tiktok.com/discover/<topic>` show captions + like counts: wait 5 s, scroll 5 ticks, then read the page text. Use the exact "Related to …" topic names; invented slugs redirect to the For You feed. Hunting/fishing/trade lingo: MeatEater, NRA Women glossary, WeldingWeb/Rokslide forums.
4. **Gift test** – who buys it for whom.

Banks: docs/research/inside-jokes-2026-10-17.html (lingo + lines per niche), report https://claude.ai/artifact/NLYJ3a9ufJXc2MX8xkHTWZ.

What the data says (Oct 2026): sarcastic restraint lines sell most (TikTok Shop "I Could Be Meaner" 4.5K sold; Etsy "things I manage not to say" 7,300 favs); embroidered sarcasm is the sweet spot; faith embroidery sells (1.9K); puns lose; personalization leads sports-mom (later project); texture (chenille/sequin) is big but not Printful-able, so we use embroidery and puff.
