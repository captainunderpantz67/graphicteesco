# SEO Audit: graphicteesco.com (2026-10-06)

**Scope:** fresh `npm run build` against live Shopify data (72 pages: 57 indexable, 15 noindex). Every page in `dist/` was checked, plus the page templates, `schema.ts`, `collections.ts`, `site.ts`, `keyword-bank.json`, `intake-map.json`, the SOP and the intake log. I spot-checked the live site with `curl`, and it matches this build: same meta, same "Midweight" text, 56 sitemap URLs.
**Method:** scripted extraction (titles, meta, H1, JSON-LD, links, alt text, 5-gram duplicate overlap, exact-match counts against the keyword bank), then a manual read of every product page's copy.
**Not done / not verifiable:** no paid API calls, so no SERP-overlap percentages, no live volumes and no Lighthouse run. Search Console index status is unknown. Product tags (`ai-art` and others) come from the intake log, not from Shopify directly. "Unmeasured" means the phrase is in the bank with `volume: null`, or isn't in the bank at all.

## Critical issues

None of these blocks indexing; robots, canonicals and the sitemap are all clean. These are the issues that will most limit ranking.

### C1. Over-40 collection is a duplicate of the women's collection (cannibalization + wrong chips)
- **Where:** `src/data/collections.ts` line 284 → `shopifyHandle: 'graphic-tees-for-women'` on the `graphic-tees-for-women-over-40` entry.
- **Evidence:** `/graphic-tees-for-women-over-40/` and `/graphic-tees-for-women/` both list the same 21 products. Every women's or unisex product page shows a "Graphic Tees for Women Over 40 & 50" chip. That includes pages never tagged over-40, such as Grace Wins, Ghost Club and Football Mom. The 2026-10-05 log says a real Shopify smart collection `graphic-tees-for-women-over-40` now exists, and only Sweet Tea & Sunsets is tagged into it.
- **Fix (code, 1 line):** set `shopifyHandle: 'graphic-tees-for-women-over-40'`. Then decide whether the page stays indexable with 1 product. My recommendation: keep it, because its 216 words of style-guide copy differ by intent. But tag 4–6 more women's designs over-40 (via DESIGN NOTES EXTRA TAGS) so the grid isn't thin.

### C2. Collection meta descriptions truncated mid-word (7 indexable pages)
- **Cause:** `niche()` in `collections.ts` line 190 does `.slice(0, 158)`.
- **Evidence:**
  - `/fishing-t-shirts/` "…early-morning-on-the-water scen"
  - `/hunting-t-shirts/` "…dawn-in-the-field s"
  - `/western-graphic-tees/` "…vintage feel. Printed"
  - `/country-graphic-tees/` "…rodeo art. Print"
  - `/cropped-graphic-tees/` "…high-rise jeans and sk"
  - `/vintage-graphic-tees/` "…70s–90s-inspired art. Print"
  - `/football-shirts/` "…no team marks. Pr"
  - The noindex hoodie pages have the same bug.
- **Fix (code):** write each niche description by hand at ≤155 characters, or trim at the last full sentence. Drop "Original … Original …" (both "Original fishing t-shirts" and "Original anime-style characters" appear).

### C3. Product H1s don't contain the product's keyword (all 29 product pages)
- **Cause:** `src/pages/products/[handle].astro` line 314 renders `p.title.replace(/^.*?–\s*/, '')`. The niche goes into a `<p>` underneath it.
- **Evidence:** `/products/womens-baseball-mom-diamond-days-graphic-tee/` H1 = "Diamond Days Tee"; `/products/mens-hunting-marsh-morning-graphic-tee/` H1 = "Marsh Morning Tee". The only exception is Game Day Football Mom, and only because the niche got pasted into the design name.
- **Fix (code):** H1 = design + niche + type, matching the SEO-title pattern: "Diamond Days Baseball Mom Tee", "Marsh Morning Duck Hunting Tee". Keep the big display styling on the design name with a `<span>` and put the niche in a second span inside the same H1. That is one template change for all 29 pages.

### C4. False hoodie claim on all 72 pages (SOP "no invented facts" + trust)
- **Evidence:**
  - Top banner on every page: "New designs drop every month · Every design also comes on a hoodie".
  - Homepage meta: "Every design also on a hoodie."
  - Entity statement (schema, llms.txt, About): "most tee designs also come on a hoodie".
  - 16 indexable collections: H2 "Want it warmer? It comes as a hoodie too", with the line "Most designs in this collection are also printed on hoodies".
  - Men's and women's collection FAQ: "Do the designs come on hoodies too? Yes."
  - The intake log confirms the store has **0 hoodie products**.
- **Fix (code):** gate every hoodie claim on a hoodie actually existing. For example, add a fact `hoodiesLive: boolean` in `site.ts` and use the existing `needs:` pattern for FAQs. Until then, change the banner to something true ("Original art · Printed to order"). The entity statement can't be gated, so reword it to drop the hoodie clause until hoodies ship, then restore it word-for-word.

### C5. Stuffed / word-salad product copy on the older sweep pages
Full quotes are in the stuffing section below. The worst pages are High Country Trout, Barbell Club, Turkey Bowl, Iron & Sweat, Fresh Cut Christmas Trees and Cabin Christmas. This is the owner's main complaint, and it sits on the pages where "the magic lives".
- **Fix (Shopify copy):** rewrite the Story, the Q&As and the SEO description on those 6 pages (and lightly on 4 more) to one primary phrase plus 2–3 natural variants.

### C6. "Midweight" on Bella+Canvas 3001 pages (SOP fabric rule)
- **Evidence:**
  - `/products/mens-fishing-high-country-trout-graphic-tee/`: "Midweight 4.2 oz./yd.² fabric".
  - `/products/mens-gym-barbell-club-graphic-tee/`: "Midweight 4.2 oz./yd.² fabric", plus Story "It's a midweight cotton tee", plus Q&A "a soft, midweight cotton graphic tee".
  - `/products/mens-thanksgiving-turkey-bowl-graphic-tee/`: "Midweight 4.2 oz./yd.² fabric", plus Q&A "a soft midweight cotton tee that holds up to repeat wear".
- **Fix (Shopify copy):** "Lightweight 4.2 oz/yd² (142 g/m²)" in Details, and "lightweight" (or just "soft cotton") in the Story and Q&A.
- **Not violations:**
  - Iron & Sweat and Fresh Cut Christmas Trees **no longer contain "midweight"**: both say "lightweight" in the current build. They appear to have been fixed already.
  - Plate Club says "Midweight 5.0–5.3 oz". That is a different (heavy-cotton) blank, so the SOP's 3001/6400 rule doesn't apply. I couldn't verify against Printful.

### C7. `/football-shirts/` is indexable, off-intent and thin
- **Evidence:** it targets "football shirts" (74,000 / KD51; the SERP is almost certainly jerseys and team merch, but I couldn't check SERP overlap). Its only product is Men's Thanksgiving – Turkey Bowl. Game Day Football Mom is not in it.
- **Fix:** add `football-shirts` to the noindex rule until it has 3+ real football designs. Or retarget it to a long-tail such as "vintage football shirts" or "retro football graphic tee" (unmeasured).

### Cannibalization pairs
No SERP overlap was measured (that would need paid DataForSEO). These are judged on target-phrase identity.
1. **`/graphic-tees-for-women/` ↔ `/graphic-tees-for-women-over-40/`**: identical product grids (C1). This is the only hard duplicate.
2. **Homepage ↔ `/graphic-tees-for-women/` and `/graphic-tees-for-men/`**: the homepage title is "Graphic Tees for Men & Women — Original Designs". Point the homepage at the brand and the "graphic tees" head term ("Original Graphic Tees & Hoodies | Graphic Tees Co."), and let the gender collections own their modifiers.
3. **Christmas cluster:** Gingerbread, Merry & Bright, Cabin and Fresh Cut all chase "christmas shirts for women/men", "classic christmas shirts" and "vintage christmas shirts", which is also the collection's job. Fix: give each product its design long-tail (table above) and give "christmas shirts for women" its own page (Opportunities #1).
4. **Gym cluster:** Plate Club ("men's gym shirt"), Barbell Club and Iron & Sweat all chase "mens gym shirts / classic mens gym wear / mens gym graphic tees", the same as `/mens-gym-shirts/`.
5. **Cute women's Halloween:** Pumpkin Patch Ghost and Witchy Season both lead on "cute Halloween shirt for women", and both ask "Do you have plus-size Halloween shirts for women?". Midnight Feature and Ghost Club both lead on "retro Halloween shirt".
6. **Hunting:** First Light Buck and Marsh Morning share "hunting t shirts for men" and an identical Q&A sentence.
7. **Country:** Dirt Road Radio, Sweet Tea & Sunsets and `/country-graphic-tees/` all use "country graphic tee for women". Desert Bloom and Sweet Tea share the question "Is this a good country concert graphic tee?". Western and country collections overlap; Desert Bloom is in both. That one is fine as long as each collection's copy stays distinct.
8. **Men's vs women's vs unisex:** there are no gender-split duplicates of the same design today. Every design exists once.

Product/Offer has name, image[], brand and per-variant Offer (price "29.99" USD, InStock, url, seller). Nothing Google would reject. Warnings and improvements:

2. **Variants:** 15–80 flat `Offer`s per product, with no sku, color or size. Google's recommended pattern is `ProductGroup` + `hasVariant` + `variesBy` (size, color), with a sku per variant. Alternatively, use a single `AggregateOffer` (lowPrice = highPrice = 29.99, offerCount).
3. **Merchant-listing warnings to expect in Search Console:** no `shippingDetails`, no `hasMerchantReturnPolicy`, no `priceValidUntil`, no `itemCondition`. Return policy is gated on `site.facts.returnsPolicy`, which is null.

5. **FAQPage on all 57 indexable pages.** It's valid, but since 2023 FAQ rich results only show for government and health sites. It's harmless, so keep it for answer engines. Don't expect SERP stars or dropdowns.
6. The "How do I wash this graphic tee?" FAQ is identical on all 29 product pages. Fine for users. Drop it from the schema on products if you want each page's FAQPage to be unique.
7. Article (guides): no `image`, and `dateModified = datePublished`. HowTo rich results are deprecated. Harmless.
8. `CollectionPage.about` is a plain string; schema.org expects a Thing. It's tolerated, but `{"@type":"Thing","name":…}` is cleaner.
