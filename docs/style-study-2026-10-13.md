# Style study — Etsy, 2026-10-13 (Christmas)

Searches: "retro christmas shirt", "embroidered christmas sweatshirt", "christmas shirt women".
~30 top listings (most relevant, "Popular now" / "Etsy's Pick" badges, review counts up to 113k).
Sam's direction confirmed by the data: simple + vintage. No badges, no circle/border frames.

## Two winning styles
**A. Vintage full-bleed illustration** — art fills the chest and fades/distresses out at the edges,
no frame. 1950s-style Santa face, "'Tis the Season" collage (Santa, snowman, reindeer, checkerboard),
snowy winter village scene, Santa's sleigh over a snowy valley, "It's the Most Wonderful Time" with a
tree + fireplace. Muted palette: cream, faded red, sage, navy. Stacked repeated words ("JOLLY JOLLY JOLLY").

**B. Minimal line / embroidery look** — single-weight white or cream line art, small, centered:
a ROW of 4–5 different Christmas trees (by far the #1 motif), snowflake trio, tree grid, with a small
script line under it ("Merry Christmas", "Merry & Bright", "Winter Wonderland"). Real embroidery or
printed to look stitched. Lives on dark garment-dyed colors.

## Other patterns
- **Sweatshirts dominate**: 7 of the top 8 for "christmas shirt women" are crewnecks.
- **Blanks**: Comfort Colors (1717 tee, 1566 crew) named in titles constantly; Gildan 18000 crew.
- **Colors**: pine/moss green, pepper, ivory, berry/crimson, blue jean, sand. Garment-dyed look.
- **Motifs ranked**: tree row > vintage Santa > reindeer with ornaments on antlers > snowflakes >
  winter village > sleigh. Humor is minor at the top.
- **Prices**: $6–$42 with heavy fake "% off"; sweatshirts $15–$42. Our $29.99 tee sits at the top.

## Implications for the build
- Art prompts: NO badge, circle, banner frame or hard border. Style A = edge-fade/distressed full-chest
  art; Style B = thin single-weight line art + small script, one ink color.
- Add crewneck sweatshirts for Christmas (Printful embroidery is possible on crews for style B).
- Consider Comfort Colors blanks (garment-dyed) — margin check vs $29.99.
