## Refund policy
Exchanges, Returns & Refunds

Every item is printed to order just for you.

Wrong size? One free exchange
Within 30 days of delivery, we'll send one replacement in a new size for free. You don't need to mail the first one back. Just email us your order number and the size you need.

Misprinted, damaged or defective items
If your order arrives damaged, defective or misprinted (for example, the wrong design, wrong placement or wrong item), we'll send a free replacement or give you a full refund.
- Contact us within 30 days of receiving your order.
- Include your order number and a clear photo of the problem.
- Please don't send the item back unless we ask you to.

Lost or wrong-address orders
If your package is lost in transit, contact us within 30 days of the estimated delivery date and we'll replace it. Orders shipped to an address entered incorrectly at checkout can't be refunded, so please double-check your address.

## Shipping policy
Every tee, crewneck and hoodie is printed after you order it, then shipped straight to you from our print partner.
- Free US shipping on orders of $45 or more.
- Under $45: a flat $4.95 for tees and $8.79 for crewneck sweatshirts and hoodies (US).
- Production: 2–5 business days to print and pack.
- Delivery: about 3–8 business days after it ships. Exact estimates show at checkout.
- Tracking: you'll get a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships.
- Multiple items may arrive in separate packages.
Order early for holidays and events.
