# Camo redo plan (2026-10-17)

Sam: "the camo hoodie designs are lame, we'll have to do research and make a better run at those." Redo in place: never unpublish (R1.3). Starts when fal is funded. Builders take these before new camo items.

## Why the current run is weak (contact sheet of 6 covers, 2026-10-17)
1. **Too small.** The art is a ~5 in chest patch. From 6 ft away it reads as a smudge.
2. **Camouflaged ink.** Beige, olive and brown art on camo vanishes. Contrast is the whole job on a camo blank.
3. **AI lettering.** Text rendered by the image model comes out warped ("SIT WAIT SNACK", "FLUENT IN HONK"). That is slop and fails R-no-slop.

## What sells (Etsy hunting humor, review/favorite counts as proxy)
- One bold silhouette plus 1–4 words: a running deer over "FAST FOOD" (~75k favorites) is the category's top format.
- Dad jokes about the freezer, the wife and the stand. Gift framing ("for him", "for dad") drives Q4.
- Women's hunting humor is a growing sub-niche (blind-date, trophy-wife puns).
- Simple one- or two-color prints. Vintage distress is fine; low contrast is not.
- We recreate the *format*, never their phrases or art (rulebook "Recreate proven ideas, never art").

## The new camo standard (applies to every camo item)
1. **Size:** front print 11–12 in wide (Printful hoodie front max). Or a small left-chest mark plus a full back print (12x14 in) when the joke is a sentence.
2. **Ink:** one or two colors only, chosen from white, blaze orange (#FF6A13) or cream. Every shape and letter gets a 0.15 in solid black keyline so it reads on light and dark camo alike. No beige, olive or brown art on camo.
3. **Type set by code, never by the image model.** The model draws the silhouette only (transparent PNG, no text). The words are set in a real font (Big Shoulders Display 900 or a licensed slab) by scripts/ (PIL/SVG), then composited. That ends the warped-letter problem on every product, not just camo.
4. **The joke is readable in two seconds at 10 ft.** Max 4 words on the front. Longer lines go on the back.
5. **Mockup check before publish:** the cover must show the words legibly at 300 px wide (the grid card size). If you can't read it in the contact sheet, it doesn't ship.
