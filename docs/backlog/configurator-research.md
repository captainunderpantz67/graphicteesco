Google "custom shirts", "custom dog shirt", "custom football mom shirts" (Texas location), then the leaders' customizers.

Google's own AI Overview describes the expected flow: upload a photo → pick style and size → add name/text → preview the mockup → order.

## What the sports-mom shirts need
Player name, jersey number, team name, team colors, sometimes a mascot or photo, front and back, more than one kid ("+ add another player"), senior night / class-of versions, sweatshirts and hoodies too.

Re-measure with Search Console / another source before building those pages.

## Recommendation: template-first, fill-in-the-blanks
1. Start from a finished design, never a blank shirt. Each template = its own SEO page.
2. One simple question at a time, big buttons, phone-first: Player name → Number → Team name → Team colors (tap 2 swatches) → Photo (optional; auto background removal) → Shirt color and size.
3. Live preview updates as she types, with a front/back toggle. The design auto-fits: no dragging, resizing or font menus.
4. "+ Add another player" for siblings.
5. A final "Does this look right?" check that reads back the spelling ("MATEJA #78").
6. Share link ("send to the team mom") and team pricing for bulk orders.
7. AI only where it helps: photo styling (bootleg/vintage) and background removal, max 2 regenerations.
8. Same engine for pets, photos, names and teams; only the questions change per template.
