# Brief: what Born To Be Sassy teaches us (2026-10-15)

Prepared for Sam to review when credits reset (~4:15 PM). Nothing here has been applied to the builder yet. Reply on Telegram with the numbers you approve ("yes 1 2 3 5") and the builder adds them to its rules.

## What they are
A Southern women's boutique (borntobesassy.com) that shows up everywhere in search and social for Christmas, fall and game-day shirts. They win on **look, styling and price**, not on clever designs. Their weakness: almost no wordplay on the shirts, nothing for men, and every design looks like every other boutique's.

## Our edge
**Their boutique look + our wordplay.** "Sleigh Queen" in plaid-filled letters with a bow is something they don't sell and a shopper hasn't seen 50 times. We keep the keyword discipline they don't have (one measured keyword per page) and the vintage style for men's and unisex.

## Recommendations, best first

### 1. Add a "Boutique" design style for women's holiday (high impact, no cost)
A second art style alongside the vintage full-bleed, used for **women's** holiday tees and crewnecks:
- **Patterned letters:** one big word (MERRY, JOY, GOBBLE, THANKFUL) or a short pun, filled with buffalo plaid, leopard, gingham, candy stripe or tartan.
- **Coquette bows:** red ribbon bows on the lettering, over a small motif, or on the sleeve.
- **Faux textures:** printed to look like chenille patches, embroidery, latch-hook or glitter. Sell it as a "look", never claim it's real chenille or glitter.
- **Small motif clusters:** 3 tiny embroidered-look trees, gingerbread men or pies, centered at chest height.
- **Pink and green Christmas,** plus classic red and green.
- **Thanksgiving:** rust, cream and sage on brown, rust and olive shirts.
- **Placement:** centered, mid-size (about 10 inches wide), not full-bleed.
- **Joke rule still applies:** the pun lives inside the style. Example: "Sleigh Queen" in plaid letters with a bow; "Pie Oh My" with three tiny pies; "Gobble Til You Wobble" in gingham script.

### 2. Bright shirt colors for women's (high impact, no cost)
Their best sellers sit on **hot pink, kelly green, red, chocolate brown and light pink**, not just naturals. For women's holiday products, include at least 2 bold colors per product that Printful offers on the blank, with mockups for each.

### 3. Styled, festive covers (high impact, small fal cost)
Every women's cover gets a decorated holiday room or market plus props: a cocoa mug, statement earrings, a bracelet stack, shopping bags, ornaments. For unisex, use couples or friend groups in matching shirts. Same realism bar and model registry as now.

### 4. Matching-set merchandising (medium impact, no cost)
- Build tee + crewneck pairs of the same design (already doing this).
- Add a **"Matching Family / Friends"** angle for unisex holiday designs. "matching christmas shirts" has 1,900 searches a month; "family christmas shirts" has 8,100 (KD 56).

### 5. Color dots on product cards (quick site change)
Show 4–5 small color swatches under each card in the grid, as they do. It tells shoppers at a glance that the shirt comes in their color. About 30 minutes of builder time.

### 6. Seasonal-first navigation (quick site change)
Their nav leads with New, Deals, then Christmas, Football and Fall. Ours already leads with Holidays. Add a **"New"** link (newest 24 products) so returning visitors see fresh drops.

### 7. Sale pricing with a crossed-out price (NEEDS YOUR YES, changes prices)
They show $29 crossed out to $20.30 everywhere. Options:
- **A:** Keep $29.99 / $39.99 and add no fake anchors (honest, simplest).
- **B:** A real holiday sale, e.g. tees $24.99 with compare-at $29.99 through Dec 1. That's a real price cut, so margin drops about $5 per tee (Printful base for the 3001 tee is $11.92).
- **Recommendation:** B, but only once checkout is open and we see first orders.

### 8. Email capture (later, after checkout opens)
15% off first order for email signup, then a small holiday drop email each week. Worth doing once traffic arrives. Needs a Shopify email app or Klaviyo, which is your call.

### 9. Don't copy these
- **Their logo, name, "sassy" branding, monogram products or photos.**
- **Their exact designs:** the gingerbread trio, the "Merry" patchwork, the Santa latch-hook face. We use the *techniques* (pattern fill, bows, faux textures), never their compositions.
