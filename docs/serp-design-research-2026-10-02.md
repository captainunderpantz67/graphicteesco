## Takeaways applied to the build
1. Small Shopify boutiques win most niche terms (mom, christian, gym, hunting graphic, western hoodie) — winnable.
2. Collection pages that win: keyword-rich title, intro + H2 copy, FAQ, related-collection link block, CollectionPage/Breadcrumb/ItemList schema.
3. Visual nav by theme (Buckle circles / Tillys sidebar) is the common pattern for graphic-tee catalogs.
4. Lifestyle model photography in the hero (Threadheads) — slot reserved for Printful mockup PNGs.
5. **Intent mismatches to fix (needs Sam's call):** fishing shirts/hoodie and soccer shirts are performance/jersey SERPs.
   Retarget: "fishing graphic tees"/"fishing t shirts", "hunting graphic tees", and soccer → "soccer mom shirts" (Born To Be Sassy pattern).
